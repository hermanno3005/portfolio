Chalk

Privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Chalk keeps your training on your iPhone. It has no account, no server and nothing to send home.

What Chalk stores

Your library: the exercises, ergs and gyms you create, every entry you log, your goals, your heart-rate zones and the units you pick. All of it is stored on your iPhone, in Chalk's own storage, and nowhere else. Chalk doesn't sync it to iCloud or to any other service.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing, unless you send it. When you make a backup, Chalk writes your library into a file and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes, whether Files, AirDrop or Mail, is up to you, and Chalk never sees it again. A restore reads a file you pick, on your iPhone.

What Chalk doesn't have

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting of its own.
  • No ads.
  • No third-party code. Chalk is built from Apple's frameworks alone.

Chalk collects no data, in the App Store's sense of the word.

Siri, Spotlight and Shortcuts

So that you can find them from Spotlight and Shortcuts, iOS indexes the names of your exercises and ergs on your iPhone. Siri and Shortcuts answer from the library on your iPhone. What you say to Siri is handled by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, not by Chalk. Likewise, if you turn on Share with App Developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports from Chalk.

Your iPhone's backups

If your iPhone backs itself up to iCloud or to a computer, those backups include Chalk's data, as they include every app's. Your Apple settings govern them, and Chalk can't read them.

Deleting your data

Deleting Chalk deletes everything it stores. Make a backup first if you want to keep your history.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date at the top. Chalk is made by Hermann Aust. Questions about your privacy go to chalk@hermann-aust.com.
